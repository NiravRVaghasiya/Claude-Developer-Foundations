# Certification Integrity

This repository must contain original educational material and original practice questions.

Never:
- reproduce real exam questions
- reconstruct recalled questions
- claim a question appeared on the exam
- imply access to confidential exam content
- disguise recalled material as original

Clearly distinguish:
- official source material
- original study explanations
- original practice questions
- secondary/community interpretation

Use "practice question", "study question", or "original question" for generated assessment content.
